# Excel Sales Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard that analyses a full year of sales (January to December 2025). It turns 490 raw transactions into KPIs, charts and a slicer, so profit can be explored by product, category, sales channel, customer segment and region.

![Dashboard preview](dashboard-preview.png)

## Project Overview

The dashboard answers key business questions:
- How much revenue, cost and profit did the business make?
- Which products and categories drive the most profit?
- Which sales channel and customer segment perform best?
- How does profit change over the year, and does it differ by region?

## Dataset

The `Data` sheet holds 490 sales records with these columns:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Date | Date of sale (2025) |
| Customer Segment | Consumer, Corporate or Small Business |
| Product | Product A to E |
| Category | Tech, Furniture or Office Supplies |
| Sales Channel | Online or Retail |
| Region | North, South, East or West |
| Units | Number of units sold |
| Revenue / Cost / Profit | Financial values per transaction |

## Dashboard Features

- **KPI cards:** Total Revenue, Total Cost, Total Profit, Average Units and Top Product
- **Line chart:** Profit over time
- **Column charts:** Profit by Product and Profit by Category
- **Doughnut charts:** Profit by Sales Channel and by Customer Segment
- **Region slicer:** filters the whole dashboard at once
- **10 PivotTables** feeding every KPI and chart

## Key Insights

- Total revenue was **$749,907**, with a total profit of **$262,764** (about a 35% profit margin).
- **Product D** is the top performer, contributing **$124,533** in profit (about 47% of the total).
- **Tech** is the most profitable category, ahead of Furniture and Office Supplies.
- **Retail** generates more profit than Online.
- Profit stays stable through the year, with a strong increase in December.

## Tools and Skills Used

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Slicers for interactive filtering
- KPI cards and dashboard layout design
- Data visualisation (line, column and doughnut charts)

## How to Use

1. Download `Excel_Dashboard_Tutorial_-_Completed.xlsx`.
2. Open it in Microsoft Excel (desktop version recommended, since slicers work best there).
3. Open the **Dashboard** sheet and use the **Region** slicer to filter the results.
